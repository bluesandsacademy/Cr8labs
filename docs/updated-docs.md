How it works
Every AR kit ships ready to run. Three steps, about five minutes.

1. Set up
   Download the ARpedia app, mount your charged tablet in the Spotty stand on a flat, well lit table, and plug in the built in cable.
2. Calibrate
   Open the app, allow camera and microphone access, and follow the prompts so Spotty can see the table clearly. Keep the space in front of it clear.
3. Play
   Lay the book flat under the camera and open it. The app recognises the page and the story starts. Place the paper tokens where prompted and watch them trigger animations, games and 3D graphics on screen.
   Works fully offline once installed. No account, no subscription, no setup fee.
