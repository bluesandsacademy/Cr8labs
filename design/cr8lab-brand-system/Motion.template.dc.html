Motion principles

Micro-interactions

150 to 180ms, ease-out. Buttons lift, cards lift and glow, links get an underline that draws in. Nothing scales or rotates on hover; that's reserved for loading and the scroll portal.

Page reveals

400 to 600ms ease-out, staggered 60 to 80ms per item. Hero text and the portal panel are the one exception: they're present on load, not animated in, since the scroll sequence is the moment that carries motion on that page.

Scroll-linked

Driven by scroll progress, never by a timer. Home-page portal only, not a pattern repeated on inner pages. Falls back to a plain opacity crossfade when scroll-timeline isn't supported.

Reduced motion

Every scroll-linked or looping animation either collapses to a static crossfade or slows substantially. Nothing that conveys information depends on motion alone.
